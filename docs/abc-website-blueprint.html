What happens when someone pays

  1. The donor opens the link and picks an amount.
  2. Our server asks Razorpay to create an order for exactly that amount.
  3. The Razorpay window opens. The donor pays by UPI, card or netbanking.
  4. Razorpay notifies our server, and the server checks the payment is genuine.
  5. The donation is saved and the donor gets a thank-you email with a receipt.
  6. Money reaches the association's bank account in 2–3 working days.

How it's built

Simple parts, mostly free

Running costs are mainly the domain (about ₹1,000 a year) plus Razorpay's fee of about 2% per donation. Hosting, the database, the blog editor and email all fit in free plans at ABC's size.
